Privacy Policy

Easy Flashcards — Last updated: September 22, 2026

In summary: Easy Flashcards does not collect, store, or share your personal data. All content remains saved exclusively on your device.

1. Data Controller

Easy Flashcards is developed and published by P0liz (hereinafter "we", "us", or "the Controller"). For any privacy-related requests, you can contact us via email at: p0liz.dev@gmail.com.

2. Data Collected

We do not collect any personal data. The application does not require registration, does not manage user accounts, and does not collect personal or identifiable information (such as name, email address, geographic location, or device identifiers).

All created content (decks, flashcards, study sessions, and progress) is stored locally on the user's device via local storage (AsyncStorage) and is never sent to servers owned by us.

3. AI Features and Third-Party Services

When you use the optional automatic flashcard generation feature powered by artificial intelligence, the text you choose to process is transmitted to the Google Gemini API via a secure HTTPS connection.

  • The text is transmitted solely for the purpose of extracting and generating the requested flashcards.
  • Our backend acts as a simple forwarding proxy and does not store or track the submitted text.
  • The processing of the text is subject to Google's terms and policies: Google Gemini API Terms of Service .
  • No data is retained once the generation is complete.

4. Advertising and Tracking

Easy Flashcards does not include advertisements, profiling SDKs, tracking tools, or third-party analytics platforms.

5. Data Security

All network communications required for AI generation use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS / TLS). Because your study data resides solely within your device's local storage, it is not exposed to remote security risks.

6. User Rights and Deletion

Because we do not collect data on remote servers, you have full and total control over all your data. You can delete any deck or flashcard directly within the application at any time. Uninstalling the application or clearing the app data from your device settings will permanently remove all saved data.

7. Children's Privacy

The application is designed for a general audience aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this Privacy Policy will be communicated by updating this page and modifying the "Last updated" date.
