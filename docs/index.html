Privacy Policy

Easy Flashcards — Last updated: November 27, 2026

In summary: Easy Flashcards does not collect, store, or share your personal data on our servers. All study content remains saved exclusively on your device.

1. Data Controller

Easy Flashcards is developed and published by P0liz (hereinafter "we", "us", or "the Controller"). For any privacy-related requests, you can contact us via email at: p0liz.dev@gmail.com.

2. Data Collected and Local Storage

We do not collect any personal data. The application does not require registration, does not manage user accounts, and does not collect personal or identifiable information (such as name, email address, geographic location, or device identifiers).

All created content (decks, flashcards, attached images, study sessions, and progress) is stored locally on the user's device via local storage (AsyncStorage and device file storage) and is never sent to databases owned by us.

3. AI Features and Third-Party Providers

When you use the optional automatic flashcard generation or remediation features powered by artificial intelligence, the study material you choose to process (text, images, or PDF documents) is transmitted via a secure HTTPS connection to third-party AI providers—currently OpenAI and Google (Gemini).

  • The submitted material is processed solely to extract text, crop figures, generate embeddings for duplicate detection, and create the requested flashcards.
  • Our backend acts solely as a temporary forwarding and processing proxy and does not store, log, or retain your submitted text, images, or PDFs once the request is complete.
  • The processing of submitted data is subject to the respective terms and privacy policies of the AI providers: OpenAI Privacy Policy and Google Gemini API Terms of Service .

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

If you subscribe to the optional Plus plan, billing and payment processing are handled exclusively by your device's official app marketplace (Google Play or the Apple App Store). We never collect or have access to your payment card or billing details.

To verify active subscription entitlements and manage monthly AI generation quotas, the App uses RevenueCat with an anonymous app-generated identifier (not linked to your name or email). For more details, see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising and Tracking

Easy Flashcards does not include advertisements, profiling SDKs, tracking tools, or third-party analytics platforms.

6. Data Security

All network communications required for AI generation and subscription verification use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS / TLS). Because your study data resides solely within your device's local storage, it is not exposed to remote database breaches.

7. User Rights and Deletion

Because we do not collect personal data on remote servers, you have full and total control over all your data. You can delete any deck or flashcard directly within the application at any time. Uninstalling the application or clearing the app data from your device settings will permanently remove all locally saved data.

8. Children's Privacy

The application is designed for a general audience aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this Privacy Policy will be communicated by updating this page and modifying the "Last updated" date.
