Privacy Policy

Easy Flashcards — Last updated: November 27, 2026

In summary: Easy Flashcards does not require an account and stores your decks and study progress exclusively on your device. This Privacy Policy is drafted in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and applicable Italian data protection law (D.Lgs. 196/2003, as amended by D.Lgs. 101/2018).

1. Data Controller

Easy Flashcards is developed and published by P0liz (hereinafter "we", "us", or "the Controller"). For any privacy-related requests, you can contact us via email at: p0liz.dev@gmail.com.

2. Data Collected and Local Storage

We do not collect personal registration data. The application does not require sign-up, does not manage user accounts, and does not collect identifying information such as your name, email address, or geographic location.

All created content (decks, flashcards, attached images, study sessions, and progress) is stored locally on your device via local storage (AsyncStorage and device file storage) and is never saved to databases owned by us.

3. Device Permissions

To support optional features, the App may request the following device permissions only when you trigger the corresponding action:

  • Camera: to take photos of study notes or pages to attach to flashcards or convert into cards with AI.
  • Photo Library / Media: to select existing images from your device to attach to flashcards or use for AI generation.
  • Document / File Access: to import PDF files you choose to process.
  • Notifications (optional): to deliver local study reminders, if enabled by you.

You can grant, manage, or revoke any of these permissions at any time in your device's system settings.

4. AI Features and Third-Party Providers

When you use the optional automatic flashcard generation or remediation features powered by artificial intelligence, the study material you choose to process (text, images, or PDF documents) is transmitted via a secure HTTPS connection to third-party AI providers—currently OpenAI and Google (Gemini).

  • The submitted material is processed solely to extract text, crop figures, generate embeddings for duplicate detection, and create the requested flashcards.
  • Our backend acts solely as a forwarding and processing proxy and does not store or retain your submitted study content (text, images, or PDFs) in any application database once the request is complete.
  • However, our hosting and network infrastructure (as well as standard server error logs) may temporarily record minimal technical metadata—such as IP addresses, timestamps, and HTTP status codes—for a limited retention period strictly necessary for security, abuse prevention, rate limiting, and troubleshooting.
  • The processing of submitted data by the AI providers is subject to their respective privacy policies and API terms: OpenAI Privacy Policy and Google Gemini API Terms of Service .

5. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

If you subscribe to the optional Plus plan, billing and payment processing are handled exclusively by your device's official app marketplace (Google Play or the Apple App Store). We never collect or have access to your payment card or billing details.

To verify active subscription entitlements and manage monthly AI generation quotas, the App uses RevenueCat with an anonymous app-generated identifier (not linked to your name or email). For more details, see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

6. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR Art. 6 & Art. 13)

Where European data protection law applies, we process personal data only on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): transmitting your selected study materials to AI providers (OpenAI, Google Gemini) when you actively request flashcard generation or remediation, and verifying your Plus subscription entitlement via Google Play / Apple App Store and RevenueCat.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): processing minimal technical infrastructure logs (such as IP address and request metadata) for a limited time to ensure service security, prevent API abuse, and diagnose technical errors.
  • Compliance with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): fulfilling applicable tax, accounting, or consumer protection obligations tied to in-app purchases where required by law.

7. International Data Transfers

Our third-party service providers—specifically OpenAI, Google, and RevenueCat—are headquartered in or operate infrastructure in the United States (outside the European Economic Area). When you use AI features or subscription verification, data may be transferred to the United States in accordance with Chapter V of the GDPR, relying on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF) where applicable and/or Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission.

8. Advertising and Tracking

Easy Flashcards does not include advertisements, behavioral profiling SDKs, tracking tools, or third-party marketing analytics platforms.

9. Data Security

All network communications required for AI generation and subscription verification use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS / TLS). Because your study data resides solely within your device's local storage, it is not exposed to remote database breaches.

10. Your Rights Under the GDPR (Arts. 15–22) and Data Deletion

Because your decks and study data reside locally on your device, you have direct control over them: you can edit or delete any deck or flashcard inside the App at any time, and uninstalling the App or clearing app storage from your device settings permanently removes all locally saved data.

Under the GDPR, you also have the right to request:

  • Access (Art. 15): confirmation of whether personal data concerning you is being processed and a copy of such data.
  • Rectification (Art. 16) and Erasure (Art. 17) of inaccurate or no longer necessary data.
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18) and Objection (Art. 21) to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Data portability (Art. 20) where technically applicable and based on contract or consent.
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights (for example, regarding temporary technical logs or anonymous RevenueCat identifiers), contact us at p0liz.dev@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority, in particular the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it) or the supervisory authority of your EU Member State of residence.

11. Children's Privacy

The application is intended for users aged 14 and older (the minimum age for digital consent in Italy pursuant to D.Lgs. 101/2018; if your country sets a higher age threshold, that threshold applies unless a parent or legal guardian provides consent). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the applicable age threshold.

Any updates to this Privacy Policy will be communicated by updating this page and modifying the "Last updated" date.
